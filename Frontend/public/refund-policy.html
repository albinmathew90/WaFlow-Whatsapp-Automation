3. Subscription Plans

WA Flow is offered as a subscription-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform.

Current subscription plans include:

  • Monthly Plan: ₹249 per month
  • Yearly Plan: ₹1,499 per year

Subscription fees are charged in advance for the selected billing cycle.

4. 24-Hour Free Trial

To help users evaluate WA Flow before purchasing a subscription, eligible new users may receive a 24-hour free trial.

During the trial, users are encouraged to:

  • Explore platform features
  • Test automation workflows
  • Create broadcast campaigns
  • Use AI-powered marketing tools
  • Evaluate dashboard functionality
  • Determine whether WA Flow meets their business needs

At the end of the trial, continued access to premium features requires the purchase of an active subscription.

We reserve the right to modify, restrict, or discontinue the free trial at any time and to deny repeated or abusive trial registrations.

5. No Refund Policy

WA Flow operates under a strict No Refund Policy.

Because users are provided with a free trial before purchasing, all subscription payments are final.

Once payment has been successfully processed:

  • No refunds will be issued.
  • No partial refunds will be issued.
  • No credits will be issued for unused time.
  • No pro-rata refunds will be provided.

This policy applies to both monthly and yearly subscription plans.

6. Situations Where Refunds Will Not Be Provided

Refund requests will not be approved for reasons including, but not limited to:

  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Failure to use the platform
  • Accidental purchase
  • Purchase made without fully reviewing platform features
  • Early cancellation
  • Business closure
  • Lack of technical knowledge
  • Failure to achieve expected business results
  • AI-generated content not meeting expectations
  • Platform features not matching personal preferences
  • WhatsApp account suspension or restriction due to user activity
  • Violation of WhatsApp policies
  • User configuration errors
  • Failure to connect or maintain a WhatsApp account
  • Internet connectivity issues
  • Device compatibility issues outside WA Flow's control
  • Third-party service interruptions
  • Failure to cancel before renewal (where auto-renewal is enabled)

7. Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your subscription at any time.

Cancellation will:

  • Stop future renewals (where applicable)
  • Keep your subscription active until the end of the current billing period
  • Prevent future charges after the current subscription expires

Cancellation does not entitle you to a refund for the current subscription period.

8. Auto-Renewal

If your subscription is set to renew automatically, the applicable subscription fee will be charged at the beginning of each renewal period unless you cancel before the renewal date.

It is your responsibility to manage your subscription and cancel it before the renewal date if you do not wish to continue using WA Flow.

9. Failed Payments

If a subscription payment fails:

We may:

  • Retry the payment
  • Temporarily restrict premium features
  • Suspend your account until payment is successfully received
  • Cancel the subscription if payment remains unsuccessful

Outstanding dues must be cleared before premium access can be restored.

10. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same subscription due to a verified technical error, please contact us within 7 days of the transaction.

After reviewing the payment records, we may:

  • Refund the duplicate payment, or
  • Apply the duplicate amount as credit toward your subscription, where appropriate.

This provision applies only to verified duplicate transactions caused by technical or billing errors.

11. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a payment was made without your authorization, you should:

  • Contact your payment provider immediately.
  • Notify WA Flow at waflow@devicedoctorindia.com with relevant transaction details.

We will reasonably cooperate with payment providers during the investigation of genuine unauthorized transactions.

12. Chargebacks

Initiating an unjustified chargeback or payment dispute after receiving access to WA Flow may result in:

  • Immediate suspension of your account
  • Termination of your subscription
  • Restriction of future access to WA Flow
  • Recovery of unpaid amounts through lawful means

We encourage users to contact our support team before initiating any payment dispute.

13. Promotional Offers

From time to time, WA Flow may offer promotional pricing, discounts, coupons, or special offers.

Unless expressly stated otherwise:

  • Promotional purchases are non-refundable.
  • Promotional pricing cannot be applied retrospectively.
  • Promotional discounts cannot be exchanged for cash or credits.

14. Platform Downtime

While we strive to maintain reliable service availability, temporary interruptions may occur due to:

  • Scheduled maintenance
  • Security updates
  • Cloud infrastructure issues
  • Internet outages
  • Third-party service disruptions
  • Force majeure events

Temporary downtime does not entitle users to refunds or subscription extensions unless otherwise required by applicable law.

15. WhatsApp-Related Limitations

WA Flow provides software tools for WhatsApp automation but does not control WhatsApp's infrastructure or policies.

Refunds will not be issued due to:

  • WhatsApp account bans
  • WhatsApp quality rating reductions
  • Message delivery failures caused by WhatsApp
  • Meta policy enforcement actions
  • Business verification delays
  • Third-party restrictions

Users are solely responsible for complying with WhatsApp's Terms of Service and Business Messaging Policies.

16. Service Modifications

We may add, improve, modify, or discontinue features as part of ongoing platform development.

Such changes do not constitute grounds for refunds, provided the core subscription service continues to be available.

17. Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Device Doctor India shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, or special damages arising from subscription purchases or use of WA Flow.

Any liability related to billing shall not exceed the amount paid by the user for the applicable subscription period.

18. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to update this Refund & Cancellation Policy at any time to reflect:

  • Changes in business operations
  • Legal or regulatory requirements
  • Platform updates
  • Billing process changes

The updated version will be published on the WA Flow website with a revised "Last Updated" date.
