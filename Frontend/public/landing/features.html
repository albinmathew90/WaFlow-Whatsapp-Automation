Platform Features

Everything you need to automate WhatsApp

A comprehensive breakdown of all the features available in our platform.

1. Unified Inbox & Messaging

Centralize all your customer interactions in one seamless interface.

  • Manage all WhatsApp conversations instantly in a unified, beautiful inbox where messages are sent and received in real-time via WebSockets.
  • Send and receive images, documents, videos, and audio notes directly from the dashboard.
2. Chatbot Automation

Automate your customer interactions using intelligent rule-based bots.

  • Automatically start specific workflows or send replies based on exact, partial, or regex keyword matches that customers send in the chat.
  • Set up instantaneous automated replies for out-of-office hours, welcome messages, or common FAQs.
3. Visual Flow Builder

Design advanced conversational logic without writing any code.

  • Use an intuitive visual drag-and-drop canvas to create multi-step chatbots and decision trees.
  • Build conversational routing by linking nodes, triggering API requests, and applying conditional logic.
4. Broadcast Engine & Marketing

Run massive, targeted WhatsApp marketing campaigns.

  • Send promotional bulk messages to thousands of contacts simultaneously.
  • Plan your broadcasts in advance by scheduling them for specific dates and times.
  • Monitor the success of your campaigns in real-time with Sent, Delivered, and Read metrics.
5. Contact Management

Efficiently manage and organize your customer data through various import methods.

  • Easily migrate large contact lists by importing or exporting CSV files.
  • Seamlessly synchronize your contacts directly from Google Contacts to keep your address book up to date.
  • Quickly add new leads or contacts manually on the fly.
  • Organize and segment your contacts for targeted marketing.
6. Multi-Device & Session Management

Scale your operations across multiple numbers.

  • Easily link and manage multiple WhatsApp numbers and sessions from a single dashboard.
  • Connect WhatsApp instances instantly by scanning QR codes directly within the web UI.
  • Monitor the active status and connection health of all connected WhatsApp instances.
7. Real-Time Analytics & Dashboard

Make data-driven decisions with detailed insights.

  • View message delivery rates, read rates, and active conversation volumes at a glance.
  • Track session statuses and engagement trends using interactive visual charts on the home dashboard.
8. Advanced Anti-Ban Safeguards

Maximize the safety of your WhatsApp numbers and significantly reduce the risk of bans with our proprietary protection systems.

  • Utilize sophisticated, hidden algorithms to process and deliver messages in the safest way possible.
  • Rely on industry-leading built-in safeguards that work automatically in the background to keep numbers secure during bulk operations.
  • Intelligently manage how interactions happen to ensure campaigns look natural and compliant with standard usage patterns.
9. Health Monitor

A dedicated dashboard to track system vitals and keep your platform running smoothly.

  • It tracks critical metrics like Delivery Rate, Read Rate, Reply Rate, and Stuck Rate.
  • It includes a visual AreaChart that plots your message performance over time (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or a custom date).
  • It identifies specific contacts whose messages have been stuck at "Sent" for more than 24 hours (dead or inactive numbers).
10. OTP Builder

Secure your external applications using WhatsApp authentication.

  • Build flows to generate and send One-Time Passwords via WhatsApp to authenticate users.
